# Photo credits

All photos here are from the University of Swat's own website, [uswat.edu.pk](https://uswat.edu.pk), and are
credited on the site as **"Photo: University of Swat"**, linked to the page each was published on (see
`../../data/photos.ts`). Copies are at most 1920px wide, re-encoded as JPEG without metadata; the seed
converts them to WebP.

Picked for sharpness and light, at least 800px wide. Posters, notices and text-heavy images were skipped: the
Allama Iqbal Day notification, the KPEF scholarship poster and the One Planet One Health conference poster.
Those posts, and HEC DLSEI 3.0 Cohort 4 (only a flow-chart graphic), get a text-free branded gradient.
